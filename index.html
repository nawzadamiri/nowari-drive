Nowari Drive

Twenty square kilometres of real Tampere, from Pispala to Messukylä, built as a table-top miniature. It starts as a pencil sketch: wherever you drive, walk or fly, the city is painted in. Collect passport stamps at real places all over the city, from the Cathedral to the top of Näsinneula and the oldest church in Tampere.
